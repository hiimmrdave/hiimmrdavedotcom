I spent a big chunk of time this week dealing with the snow that hit my whole region. Luckily for me, I'm not _from_ this region. I know how to handle snow.

## Week ending 31 January

The kids were home from school all week. The roads were mostly passable by Tuesday, but sidewalks weren't clear for students who walk to school, and the rural roads have taken longer to clear. I love my children, I do, but they need to go back to some structure and routine. _I_ need them to.

I hate the margins. I was troubleshooting something with my blog template, and I realized that I hate my horizontal margins. I still need to decide what I want, but it needs to be _different_.
